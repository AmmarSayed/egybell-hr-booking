# HR Appointment Booking Portal – How to use it

Employees use the portal to book a 15-minute, in-person appointment with HR. HR uses it to manage the schedule and see who has booked.

## Links

- **Employee booking page:** https://egybell-hr-appointments.onrender.com
- **HR administration page:** https://egybell-hr-appointments.onrender.com/admin (password shared separately)

## The rules

- Only **@afreximbank.com** work emails can book. Each employee confirms their email with a one-time code, so nobody can book on someone else's behalf.
- **One appointment per employee.**
- Appointments must be booked **at least 24 hours in advance**.
- Employees can **cancel their own appointment up to 24 hours before** it and then pick a new time. Within the last 24 hours, changes go through HR.
- All times are **Cairo local time**. Appointments are **15 minutes**, in person.
- Employees should **bring their national ID** for contract signing.

## How employees book

1. Open the booking page and click **Continue with work email**.
2. Enter their @afreximbank.com email, then type the **one-time code** sent to that inbox. No password or registration is needed.
3. Enter a **phone number** (with country code) for HR coordination.
4. Choose a **day**, **meeting room** and **time**, then review and click **Confirm appointment**.
5. They receive a **booking reference** on screen and can **download the confirmation image** or take a screenshot. **No confirmation email is sent.**

If an employee signs in again later, the portal shows their existing appointment and lets them download the confirmation again. If the appointment is more than 24 hours away, they will also see a **Cancel appointment** button. They can sign out from the top-right corner.

## HR administration page

Sign in with the administrator password. You will be signed out after 8 hours, or whenever the system is updated. Just sign in again.

### 1. Appointment schedule

- Each row is a **date + meeting room + start and end time** (Cairo time). The portal splits each row into 15-minute slots automatically.
- Use **Add room and date** to open more slots, or the bin icon to remove a row. Then click **Save schedule**.
- Times must be on 15-minute boundaries (e.g. 09:00, 09:15), and rows for the same room and date must not overlap.
- Removing a row closes its free slots. Appointments already booked in it are **kept** and still appear in the bookings list.
- If you see "The schedule changed", someone else saved in the meantime. Refresh the page and make your change again.

### 2. Bookings

- The table lists every booking: date, time, room, work email, phone number and reference.
- Use **From / To** and **Apply filter** to show a date range.
- Click **Download CSV** to export the current list to Excel.
- Click the **red bin icon** on a row to **delete** a booking, for example when an employee asks for a change within the last 24 hours. This frees the slot and lets the employee book again. **Deleting cannot be undone**, so download the CSV first if you want a record.

## Common questions from employees

- **"I didn't get the code."** Check the spam/junk folder and wait a minute. Make sure the email address is typed correctly.
- **"It won't accept my email."** Only @afreximbank.com addresses are allowed. Personal emails will not work.
- **"I can't see any times."** Times less than 24 hours away and already-booked times are hidden. Try another room or day, or ask HR to open more slots.
- **"I need to change my appointment."** More than 24 hours before, the employee can cancel and rebook themselves. Within 24 hours, HR deletes the booking from the admin page so they can book again.
- **"There is a 'Development mode' label on the sign-in box."** This is expected for now and doesn't affect bookings.

## Privacy

Phone numbers are visible only to HR on the admin page and in the CSV export. They are not shown to other employees or on the confirmation image. Please keep the administrator password within the HR team and store exported CSV files securely.
