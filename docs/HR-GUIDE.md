# HR Appointment Booking Portal – How to use it

Employees use the portal to book a 15-minute, in-person appointment with HR. The system is administered by Ammar Sayed: HR sends him any schedule changes, booking changes or report requests.

## Link

- **Employee booking page:** https://egybell-hr-appointments.onrender.com

## The rules

- Only **@afreximbank.com** work emails can book. Each employee confirms their email with a one-time code, so nobody can book on someone else's behalf.
- **One appointment per employee.**
- Appointments must be booked **at least 24 hours in advance**.
- Employees can **cancel their own appointment up to 24 hours before** it and then pick a new time. Within the last 24 hours, changes go through HR, who forward them to Ammar.
- All times are **Cairo local time**. Appointments are **15 minutes**, in person.
- Employees should **bring their national ID** for contract signing.

## How employees book

1. Open the booking page and click **Continue with work email**.
2. Enter their @afreximbank.com email, then type the **one-time code** sent to that inbox. No password or registration is needed.
3. Enter a **phone number** (with country code) for HR coordination.
4. Choose a **day**, **meeting room** and **time**, then review and click **Confirm appointment**.
5. They receive a **booking reference** on screen and can **download the confirmation image** or take a screenshot. **No confirmation email is sent.**

If an employee signs in again later, the portal shows their existing appointment and lets them download the confirmation again. If the appointment is more than 24 hours away, they will also see a **Cancel appointment** button. They can sign out from the top-right corner.

## What HR can request from Ammar

- **Booking list:** an up-to-date list of all bookings (date, time, room, work email, phone number, reference) as an Excel/CSV file, for any date range.
- **Schedule changes:** opening new dates, rooms or hours, or closing them. Please send the date, meeting room and start and end times (Cairo time, on 15-minute boundaries). Existing bookings are kept when hours are closed.
- **Booking changes within 24 hours:** removing an employee's booking so they can choose a new time. Please send the employee's work email or booking reference.

## Common questions from employees

- **"I didn't get the code."** Check the spam/junk folder and wait a minute. Make sure the email address is typed correctly.
- **"It won't accept my email."** Only @afreximbank.com addresses are allowed. Personal emails will not work.
- **"I can't see any times."** Times less than 24 hours away and already-booked times are hidden. Try another room or day. HR can ask Ammar to open more slots.
- **"I need to change my appointment."** More than 24 hours before, the employee can cancel and rebook themselves. Within 24 hours, HR asks Ammar to remove the booking so they can book again.
- **"There is a 'Development mode' label on the sign-in box."** This is expected for now and doesn't affect bookings.

## Privacy

Phone numbers are visible only to the administrator and in booking lists sent to HR. They are not shown to other employees or on the confirmation image. Please store booking lists securely and share them only within HR.

For any technical issue, please contact Ammar Sayed.
